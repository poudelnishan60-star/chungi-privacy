चुङ्गी Chungi Rubber — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026

Chungi Rubber is a game that runs entirely on your device. This policy explains what that means for your data.

Data we collect

None. The game does not collect, store on any server, sell or share any personal information. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no in-app purchases.

Data kept on your device

Your game progress (stars, unlocked rubbers, settings and the game you are in the middle of) is saved only on your phone, so you can carry on where you left off. It never leaves your device. Uninstalling the game or clearing its storage deletes it.

Permissions

The game may use vibration for feedback on supported devices. It does not access your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos or files.

Children

The game does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions? Use the developer email shown on the game's Google Play page.
